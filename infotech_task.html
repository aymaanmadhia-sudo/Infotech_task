About ACM

DJSCE-ACM is the student chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) at DJSCE Mumbai. Running since 2007–08, we organise workshops, hackathons, coding competitions, seminars, and industry talks for students who want to build and learn beyond the classroom.
